## Demand and platform evidence

- **Pew Oct 8, 2026:** 74% very concerned gas/energy, 73% food/consumer goods, 69% healthcare, 66% housing, 48% finding jobs; 50% role of AI in economy. Survey of 5,062 US adults Sep 28–Oct 4. https://www.pewresearch.org/politics/2026/10/08/cost-concerns-dominate-as-publics-economic-ratings-turn-more-negative/
- **Fed May 2026:** prices remained the most common financial concern; 23% of renters had been behind at some point in the prior year; 14% of insured homeowners struggled to afford premiums. Survey fielded Oct 2025, released May 2026. https://www.federalreserve.gov/publications/2026-economic-well-being-of-us-households-in-2025-executive-summary.htm
- **YouTube September 2026 Culture & Trends:** 73% of surveyed U.S. online 14–44-year-olds agreed YouTube is best for deeply exploring interests; 69% had watched niche community content. Narrow expertise can travel beyond its niche when newcomer comprehension and relevance are strong. https://blog.youtube/culture-and-trends/youtube-culture-trends-modern-mainstream-research/
- **TikTok January 2026 forecast:** Reali-TEA, Curiosity Detours, Emotional ROI; viewers want authentic context, usable discoveries and value. TikTok's marketing-derived signals are not causal rules for organic YouTube Shorts. https://newsroom.tiktok.com/introducing-tiktok-next-2026-our-trend-forecast-for-marketers-for-the-year-ahead-ca?lang=en-CA
- **Metricool July 2026:** observational study 799,718 videos / 71,177 accounts; views grew while average watch duration declined. The study reports ~76% long-form views growth and ~37% decline in avg view duration, and Shorts views +127% with avg view duration ~16 seconds. Methodology/selection bias apply; not representative of every niche. https://metricool.com/youtube-trends/
- **YouTube first-party A/B testing:** up to 3 titles and/or thumbnail combos, winner based on **watch-time share**; currently not eligible for Shorts. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en
- **YouTube retention:** first 30s intro retention vs title/thumbnail promise; dips, spikes, top moments via private Studio analytics. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/9314415
- **Meta March 2026:** explicitly rewards original value-add video rather than minor remixes. https://about.fb.com/news/2026/03/rewarding-original-creators-on-facebook/
- **YouTube monetization:** mass-produced/repetitious low-value work is ineligible. https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/1311392

**Ad-profit caveat:** public views do not reveal RPM. Higher concern or virality does not automatically mean stronger ad profit; compare actual revenue and sustained watch time from *your own* channel, separately for YouTube Shorts vs long-form. Use Shorts as original discovery content and a testable funnel rather than assuming it monetizes like 15–25min films.

## Suggested research batch protocol

For each niche every 2–4 weeks: 5–10 channels with similar audience intent, 10–20 recent long videos per channel, sampled Shorts separately, visible thumbnails captured only from lawful public metadata, top and bottom performers, topic/search/survey context, original claim sources, title snapshot, published date and view observation timestamp. Produce 8–12 distinctive ideas, 3 package hypotheses for the top candidate, and a complete first-30s story + payoff. Human approve before expensive generation. No magical virality score.
